# 📈 Agentic Trading Floor (TradingAgents Architecture)

This skill operationalizes an **institutional multi-agent financial simulation framework** (Jamie Dimon fortress liquidity / Warren Buffett capital preservation doctrine). It models the adversarial, rigorous debate of Wall Street investment committees.

---

## 🎯 When to Use

* **Comprehensive Asset Due Diligence:** Evaluating equities, fixed income, or commodities with multi-angle intelligence.
* **Adversarial Thesis Testing:** Forcing a Bull vs. Bear debate to uncover blind spots and downside catalysts before allocating capital.
* **Portfolio Risk Management:** Stress-testing portfolio exposure against drawdown, volatility, and sector concentration limits.
* **Investment Committee Memos:** Synthesizing qualitative moat analysis, quantitative metrics, and macroeconomic factors into executive briefs.

---

## 🧭 The 4-Stage Trading Floor Protocol

```
1. Multi-Disciplinary Ingestion ──► 2. Adversarial Bull/Bear Debate ──► 3. Fortress Risk Gate ──► 4. Execution Sizing
```

### Stage 1: Multi-Disciplinary Ingestion
- **Fundamental Analyst:** Evaluates Owner Earnings, free cash flow conversion, ROIC, and competitive moat (Buffett).
- **Technical Analyst:** Reviews momentum, moving averages, relative strength (RSI), and support/resistance levels.
- **Sentiment Analyst:** Analyzes news tone, conference call transcripts, and social retail sentiment.

### Stage 2: Adversarial Bull/Bear Debate
- The Bull agent argues the expansionary thesis, growth catalysts, and margin expansion.
- The Bear agent challenges with debt maturity walls, regulatory headwinds, and customer churn.
- A synthesis agent reconciles divergent perspectives into an unbiased risk-reward profile.

### Stage 3: Fortress Risk Gate (Jamie Dimon)
- Enforces Value-at-Risk (VaR) caps and max drawdown rules.
- Rejects proposals that rely on excessive leverage or illiquid positions.

### Stage 4: Execution Sizing & Synthesis
- Calculates Kelly-criterion or volatility-weighted position sizing.
- Defines clear entry, profit target, and stop-loss levels.

---

## 🛡️ Risk & Compliance Guardrails

- [ ] **Capital Preservation:** Never risk more than 2% of total portfolio capital on a single speculative trade.
- [ ] **Hallucination Protection:** Verify financial ratios against official 10-K/10-Q filings or auditable market data.
- [ ] **No Unhedged Leverage:** Explicitly model worst-case tail risk before recommending derivative or margin positions.

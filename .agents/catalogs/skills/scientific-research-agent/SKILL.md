# 🧬 Scientific Research Agent (K-Dense Standard)

This skill formalizes the **systematic scientific investigation and evidence synthesis protocol** (Demis Hassabis scientific frontier / Evidence-Based Medicine standards) using validated scientific agent profiles.

---

## 🎯 When to Use

* **Systematic Literature Reviews:** Querying PubMed, bioRxiv, and IEEE for state-of-the-art evidence across technical and biomedical fields.
* **Evidence Synthesis & GRADE Scoring:** Compiling evidence tables for clinical decision support or engineering feasibility analyses.
* **Biomechanical & Orthopedic Research:** Evaluating surgical techniques, implant designs, and kinematic outcomes.
* **Patent & Prior Art Landscaping:** Searching USPTO databases for existing patent claims and white-space opportunities.

---

## 🧭 The 4-Step Scientific Synthesis Protocol

```
1. PICO Query Formulation ──► 2. Multi-Database Retrieval ──► 3. Critical Appraisal & Extraction ──► 4. Evidence Matrix Synthesis
```

### Step 1: PICO Query Formulation
- Define Population, Intervention, Comparison, and Outcome (PICO framework).
- Translate clinical/technical question into Boolean search strings with MeSH terms.

### Step 2: Multi-Database Retrieval
- Execute search across PubMed, PMC, bioRxiv, and patent databases.
- Filter by study design (RCT, meta-analysis, cohort, case series).

### Step 3: Critical Appraisal & Extraction
- Screen titles and abstracts for relevance and methodological rigor.
- Extract effect sizes, relative risks, p-values, and statistical power metrics.

### Step 4: Evidence Matrix Synthesis
- Construct structured GRADE evidence profiles.
- Formulate balanced, objective research conclusions with explicit limitations.

---

## 🛡️ Scientific Integrity Guardrails

- [ ] **Citation Verifiability:** Never fabricate or hallucinate DOIs, PMIDs, or author affiliations.
- [ ] **Evidence Hierarchy:** Clearly label non-peer-reviewed preprints vs. randomized controlled trials.
- [ ] **No Unqualified Clinical Claims:** Present synthesis as decision support for human specialists, not definitive autonomous diagnoses.

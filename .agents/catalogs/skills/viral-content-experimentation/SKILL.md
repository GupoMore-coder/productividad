# 📈 Viral Content Experimentation (Cheat-on-Content Standard)

This skill formalizes the **scientific marketing and brand positioning methodology** (Philip Kotler quantitative marketing / Bernard Arnault luxury brand preservation standard). It turns every promotional post, newsletter, or product narrative into a calibrated, data-backed experiment.

---

## 🎯 When to Use

* **Brand Positioning & Launch Copy:** Crafting high-impact announcements for More Paper and More Design.
* **Hook Optimization:** Formulating the crucial first 3 seconds / 2 lines of text to capture high-value attention.
* **Content Post-Mortems:** Analyzing why specific campaigns over- or under-performed and updating brand guidelines accordingly.
* **Newsletter & Announcement Strategy:** Maximizing open rates, read-through completion, and conversion actions.

---

## 🧭 The 4-Stage Experimentation Protocol

```
1. Premise Formulation ──► 2. 5-Vector Blind Scoring ──► 3. Hook Calibration ──► 4. Retrospective Evolution
```

### Stage 1: Premise Formulation
Identify the core insight or desire:
- Does this solve an acute pain point?
- Does it elevate the customer's perceived status, luxury, or operational efficiency?

### Stage 2: 5-Vector Blind Scoring (Pre-Publish)
Score the draft from 1 to 10 across 5 objective vectors:
1. **Curiosity Cliff:** Does the opener create an irresistible information gap?
2. **Cognitive Ease:** Is the language crisp, punchy, and free of unnecessary fluff?
3. **Emotional Resonance:** Does it evoke desire, pride, relief, or urgency?
4. **Authority & Proof:** Does it present tangible evidence, craft quality, or metrics?
5. **Call-to-Value (Not just CTA):** What does the reader immediately gain by clicking or saving?

### Stage 3: Hook Calibration (Generate 3 Variants)
Draft at least 3 distinct hook variations:
- **Variant A (Contrarian):** Challenges conventional wisdom in luxury or productivity.
- **Variant B (Data-Driven):** Anchored by a surprising statistic or tangible case study.
- **Variant C (Storytelling):** Narrates an artisanal craft detail or founder insight.

### Stage 4: Retrospective Evolution (Post-Publish)
- After 48 hours, compare actual reach/saves with predicted scores.
- Extract the winning structural pattern and add it to the brand's copy playbook.

---

## 🛡️ Brand Integrity Guardrails

- [ ] **No Clickbait or False Promises:** All hooks must deliver on their premise with genuine substance.
- [ ] **Preserve Luxury Identity:** Never compromise the prestigious brand aesthetic of More Paper or More Design for cheap, short-lived engagement spikes.

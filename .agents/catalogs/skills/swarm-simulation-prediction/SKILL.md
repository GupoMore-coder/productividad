# 🐟 Swarm Simulation & Predictive Intelligence

This skill implements digital sandbox multi-agent simulations inspired by **MiroFish**. By creating parallel social environments populated by autonomous agents with distinct incentives and psychological profiles, it models collective emergence and forecasts potential trajectories for complex decisions.

---

## 🎯 When to Use

* **Strategic Decision Stress-Testing:** Rehearsing policy changes, major product pivots, pricing updates, or rebrands before committing capital.
* **Public Relations & Crisis Modeling:** Simulating public reaction, viral outrage, or adoption curves in response to hypothetical announcements or breaking events.
* **Economic & Market Emergence:** Modeling competitive responses from rivals, suppliers, or customer cohorts to strategic shifts.
* **Creative Counterfactuals (*What-If*):** Exploring alternative historical paths or complex narrative branches with high behavioral fidelity.

---

## 🧭 The 4-Phase Swarm Simulation Protocol

```
Phase 1: Seed Material ──► Phase 2: Agent Persona Population ──► Phase 3: God-Eye Variable Injection ──► Phase 4: Emergence & Prediction Report
```

### Phase 1: Ingestion of Seed Context
* Extract core real-world facts, rules, constraints, and initial states from seed materials (briefs, financials, news, policy drafts).

### Phase 2: Persona Synthesis & Agent Population
* Instantiate diverse cohorts representing key stakeholder archetypes (e.g., *Early Adopters*, *Skeptical Regulators*, *Price-Sensitive Customers*, *Aggressive Competitors*).
* Equip each agent with:
  * Distinct risk tolerance.
  * Baseline memory and incentives.
  * Communication and decision-making logic.

### Phase 3: God-Eye Variable Injection (*The Simulation Loop*)
* Inject specific shocks, announcements, or environmental shifts into the digital sandbox.
* Run multiple communication epochs where agents interact, form coalitions, share sentiment, and react to peers.

### Phase 4: Emergence Synthesis & Risk Scorecard
Deliver an executive prediction report detailing:
1. **Consensus Trajectory:** The most probable equilibrium state ($P > 70\%$).
2. **Fat-Tail Risks:** Low-probability, high-consequence failure modes observed during iterations.
3. **Key Tipping Points:** The specific variables or triggers that flip agent behavior from positive to hostile.
4. **Pre-Emptive Mitigations:** Recommended strategic adjustments to steer the outcome toward success.

---

## 🔒 Safety & Grounding Principles

* **Simulation vs. Reality Boundary:** Clearly label all outputs as predictive probabilistic simulations, never deterministic certainty.
* **Zero Manipulation:** Never design simulation swarms to plan malicious disinformation, astroturfing, or deceptive social engineering.

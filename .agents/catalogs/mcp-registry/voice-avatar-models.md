## 🎯 Architectural Overview

Enables high-fidelity, conversational audio interactions for clinical debriefing, patient education, voice-guided surgical navigation, and clinician digital avatars:

## 🛠️ Core Technologies & Integration Patterns

### 1. Low-Latency WebRTC Pipeline (`Nutlope/self.so`)
* Full-stack architecture using Next.js App Router, Tailwind/CSS, and LiveKit WebRTC server.
* Achieves sub-300ms glass-to-glass latency combining:
  * Speech-to-Text: Deepgram Nova-2 or Whisper-large-v3-turbo.
  * LLM Core: Low-latency streaming (Groq LLaMA-3.3-70b or Gemini Flash).
  * Text-to-Speech: Cartesia Sonic or ElevenLabs Flash.

### 2. Conversational Speech Model (`SesameAILabs/csm`)
* Generates continuous conversational speech tokens directly modeling human turn-taking, natural pauses, hesitation tokens (`"uh-huh"`, `"hmm"`), and emotional modulation.
* Eliminates robotic sentence-by-sentence pacing in clinical consultations.

### 3. Zero-Shot Voice Cloning (`jasonppy/VoiceStar`)
* Clones a specialist surgeon's vocal timbre from a 3-5 second clean speech reference prompt without model fine-tuning.
* Preserves accent, prosody, and cadence for personalized patient discharge audio instructions.

### 4. Digital Persona & Avatar Modeling (`mindverse/Second-Me`)
* Maintains dynamic persona traits, behavioral style vectors, and episodic memory of the specialist.
* Emulates the surgeon's consultation bedside manner, terminology preferences, and explanation styles.

## 🔒 Ethical & Security Gate Protocol
* **Explicit Clinician Consent & Watermarking:** Audio generated via voice-cloned models must contain inaudible synthetic watermarking and explicit audio disclaimers (`"This message is generated by an AI assistant on behalf of Dr. ..."`).
* **Anti-Spoofing & Identity Verification:** Voice cloning models must never be triggered without authenticated identity access controls.
* **PHI Audio Redaction:** Real-time STT transcripts must filter patient names and dates of birth before streaming to external voice synthesis APIs.
